## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a warm, uplifting, and approachable wellness experience. Designed to transform daily exercise and holistic movement into an encouraging routine rather than an intimidating chore, the visual aesthetic blends contemporary digital minimalism with gentle retro-game charm. The pixel art monkey mascot serves as a friendly guide, infusing warmth, playfulness, and low-friction delight across user milestones.

The visual style unites generous whitespace, ultra-soft rounded surfaces, and inviting, natural warmth. The tone is encouraging, serene, and clear—rejecting the cold, high-intensity neon or clinical aesthetics common in traditional athletic apps in favor of cozy daily balance.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, sunlit atmosphere that feels comforting yet energetic:

- **Primary Accent (`#E8875B`)**: Punchy coral orange derived from the visual identity. Used for high-priority interactive cues, streak alerts, active progress states, and playful badge highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F4B942`)**: Cheerful butter yellow utilized for welcoming hero actions, primary CTA backgrounds, celebratory rings, and reward stars.
- **Base Canvas & Containers (`#FFF8E5`)**: Soft cream tone providing warm, non-glare visual grounding across expansive layouts, balanced with crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card layers.
- **Deep Neutral & Typography (`#5A4636`)**: Rich warm dark brown ensuring optimal legibility while maintaining a soft, organic contrast that avoids the sterile harshness of pure black.
- **Muted Outlines & Dividers**: Subtly transparent tints of the neutral brown (`rgba(90, 70, 54, 0.08)`) to structure surfaces without disrupting gentle visual flow.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts spirited character with immaculate readability:

- **Headlines (`Bricolage Grotesque`)**: Brings quirky, energetic flair with curved terminal cuts that reference expressive digital charm while retaining modern editorial finesse.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Be Vietnam Pro`)**: Delivers clean, approachable humanist proportions tailored for both Latin and Korean-adjacent typesetting clarity. Provides effortless scanning for workout metrics, daily logs, and nutritional reflections.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid layout is employed across desktop screens, reflowing down to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports. Generous vertical spacing creates breathing room around core wellness content and keeps interactions calm and non-overwhelming.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 1200px max-width container centered with `1.5rem` gutters and `4.5rem` sectional pacing.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Fluid edge margins of `2rem` with compact vertical pacing of `3rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: `1rem` edge padding with single-column linear stacks, utilizing sticky bottom bars for thumb-friendly routine management.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through soft, tactile tonal layering and warm ambient shadows rather than stark elevation drops:

- **Base Layer**: Soft cream canvas (`#FFF8E5`).
- **Surface Layer**: White cards (`#FFFFFF`) layered directly over cream, bordered with a faint 1px warm line (`rgba(90, 70, 54, 0.08)`).
- **Floating & Interactive**: Tactile, sun-drenched dropshadows tinted with subtle terracotta-brown hues (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(90, 70, 54, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(90, 70, 54, 0.04)`).
- **Physical Feedback**: Interactive items use a subtle translate-y depress of 2px on active press states, harmonizing with the 8-bit mascot's playful physical feedback.

## Shapes

The design system embraces ultra-friendly, pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Cards & Modals**: `2rem` (`rounded-2xl`) to `3rem` (`rounded-3xl`) boundary curvature, eliminating sharp angles and crafting a soft, tactile, cushion-like appearance.
- **Buttons, Tags & Form Fields**: Full pill rounding (`9999px`) to reinforce tactile approachability and easy tap targets.
- **Mascot Frames**: Rounded square badges (`2rem`) anchoring pixel-art illustrations inside clean modern vector containers, creating an intentional, refined contrast between retro pixels and smooth vector geometry.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Warm Butter Yellow (`#F4B942`) background with Deep Brown (`#5A4636`) text, fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `1rem 2rem`. Subtle hover lifts with an amber drop shadow.
  - *Secondary / Accent*: Coral Orange (`#E8875B`) with white text for key conversion triggers and streak celebration.
  - *Tertiary / Ghost*: Transparent fill with a warm border (`1.5px solid rgba(90, 70, 54, 0.15)`) and dark brown text.
- **Cards**:
  - Encased in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with `2rem` border radius, resting gently on the cream canvas. Features a delicate 1px warm border. Mascot cameos peek out from top-right notches or embedded status chips.
- **Chips & Tags**:
  - Pill-shaped badges filled with translucent tints of coral (`rgba(232, 135, 91, 0.12)`) or butter yellow (`rgba(244, 185, 66, 0.18)`), pairing label-sm typography for workout categories (e.g., "15-min Stretch", "Daily Routine").
- **Inputs & Text Fields**:
  - Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) white fields with inset padding of `0.875rem 1.5rem`. Smooth focus transitions shift the border to Coral Orange (`#E8875B`) with a diffuse warm focus glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Soft custom circular indicators (`24px`) with Deep Brown check marks inside Yellow/Coral fills upon completion, accompanied by a small celebratory bounce animation.
- **Daily Progress Rings & Streak Indicators**:
  - Thick, rounded radial progress tracks featuring Coral Orange progress heads, with the pixel monkey mascot resting at the center to celebrate completed milestones.
